Note: For clarity, I am using reference clauses from AS/NZS 2312.1:2014 (Paint coatings) and AS/NZS 2312.2:2014 (Hot dip galvanizing).
Common practice in NZ: most steel protection references back to AS/NZS 2312.1 System Codes (A1, B1, etc.), plus selected “colloquial” abbreviations (e.g., PUR5).
Where no direct AS/NZS code exists for a nickname (like “PUR5”), I’ve indicated Comparable to A3 or G3.

💡 Notes on Application and Subcontracting
✅ In-house: Usually applies to light alkyd or single-pack epoxy touch-ups, minor maintenance, or smaller components.
✅ Subcontracted: All galvanizing, metal spraying, intumescent, and multi-layer high-build systems almost always subcontracted in NZ.
✅ Intumescents: Require specialist certified applicators, test certificates, and compliance documentation.
