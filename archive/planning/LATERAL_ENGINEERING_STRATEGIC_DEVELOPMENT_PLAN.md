- **Daily Volume:** 3-5 jobs with 2-4 hour cycle times
- **Primary Materials:** Flats, angles, channels, RHS/SHS profiles (metric)
- **Cutting Equipment:** Manual bandsaw, grinder, plasma, oxy/acetylene
- **Future:** CNC processes planned
- **Kerf Width:** 2.4mm + 0.5mm user error
